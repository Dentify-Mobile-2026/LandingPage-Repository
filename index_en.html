Daily Clinic Challenges

Move beyond notebooks, spreadsheets, and scattered chats

Every minute spent hunting for patient records or fixing overlapping appointments takes focus away from clinical care.

Zero schedule overlaps

Dentify automatically checks procedure durations and prevents double-booking across your daily clinical slots.

Clinical safety before anesthesia

All-In-One Platform

Six modules built for the real pace of a dental clinic

Clean, minimalist interface engineered to be used effortlessly between patients.

Dynamic Smart Calendar

Review your full month with status indicators. Tap any day to inspect appointments, reschedule, or confirm visit completion.

Patients & Medical History

Complete records with ID, emergency contacts, medical alert filtering, and chronological diagnosis and medication logs.

Treatment Catalog

Standardize pricing and estimated durations across specialties (Prevention, Endodontics, Orthodontics, Surgery, Aesthetics).

Billing & Payment Methods

Record cash, card, or bank transfer payments linked directly to appointments and close visits automatically.

Clinical Inventory Control

Organize dental supplies by category, track unit costs, and spot items needing restock before they disrupt treatments.

Live Practice Dashboard

Monitor recent revenue, today's schedule, and low-stock alerts the moment you sign in.
